# Agent Bulldog

A generalized AI negotiation agent that lowers your subscription and service bills — and won't take no for an answer.

Built with Kiro for Kiroween 2026 (Frankenstein category). Connects to the services you want to negotiate (streaming, insurance, phone, internet, and more), compares prices across the industry, and negotiates better rates with customer service — threatening to cancel where that triggers a retention offer, and retrying a denied discount with a fresh angle.

Fresh build for Kiroween 2026. Specs live in `.kiro/specs/`.
